═══════════════════════════════════════
HOW TO SCORE HOOK STRENGTH (1–10)
═══════════════════════════════════════
Ask: after reading the first tweet/sentence, could the reader stop?
  10: Impossible to stop. The first line creates an unresolved tension.
  8–9: Strong tension. Most readers continue.
  6–7: Interesting, but no urgency. Some readers stop.
  4–5: Mildly interesting. Many readers stop.
  1–3: No tension. Could be the opening of any generic post.

WHAT DESTROYS HOOK STRENGTH:
- Opening with a vague claim ("This changes everything")
- Opening with a question anyone could answer ("Why do companies fail?")
- Opening with meta-commentary about the thread ("A thread on X:")
- Using any of the prohibited opener patterns

═══════════════════════════════════════
HOW TO SCORE VOICE MATCH (1–10)
═══════════════════════════════════════
Compare against the writer's examples above, not against generic "good writing."

  10: Read the draft next to the examples — they're indistinguishable.
  8–9: Clearly sounds like this person. Occasional word choices slightly off.
  6–7: Captures the general style but missing the specific texture.
  4–5: Generic quality writing that could be anyone.
  1–3: Sounds like a different person entirely, or like AI-generated text.

CHECK THESE SPECIFICALLY:
- Sentence length patterns: does this match how they typically write?
- Punctuation habits: do they use em-dashes? parentheses? no semicolons?
- Vocabulary level: do they use jargon? which kind?
- Hedging patterns: how do they express uncertainty?
- Structural habits: how do they open and close arguments?

═══════════════════════════════════════
REWRITE INSTRUCTION FORMAT — BE SURGICAL
═══════════════════════════════════════
BAD: "Make the hook stronger. The voice needs work."
GOOD: "Tweet 1: Opening line 'Most companies miss this...' violates their pattern —
  they never open with 'Most companies.' Looking at their examples, they open with
  a specific number or a named situation stated as fact. Replace with:
  '[Specific number] of [named companies] [specific failure]. The reason isn't what you think.'
  Reference claim_id [X] for the statistic."

Every rewrite instruction must:
1. Quote the exact text to change
2. Explain why it's wrong in terms of this specific writer's patterns
3. Provide a concrete alternative or direction
